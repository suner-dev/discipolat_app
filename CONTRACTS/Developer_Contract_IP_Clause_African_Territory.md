INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONFIDENTIALITY CLAUSE - DISCIPOLAT

This clause is intended to be integrated into any employment or prestation contract
between [Organization Name] and a Developer.

1. WORK PRODUCT OWNERSHIP
   All work product, code, configurations, scripts, and documentation created by Developer 
   in relation to Discipolat Church OS shall be the exclusive property of 
   [Organization Name / ART4US]. Developer hereby assigns all rights to ART4US.

2. DISCIPOLAT-SPECIFIC ASSIGNMENTS
   - All multi-tenant architecture modifications remain property of ART4US
   - All tenant isolation mechanisms (PostgreSQL schema partitioning) remain property
   - All seed data configurations (Flyway V__seed_data.sql) remain property
   - All integrations with operator payment gateways (MTN/Orange/MPesa) remain property

3. NO OPEN SOURCE INCORPORATION
   Developer shall not incorporate any Open Source Software into Discipolat codebase 
   without written approval from ART4US. Must document any existing OSS components used.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS
   - Protect all non-public Discipolat data: church member contributions, 
     membership details, donation records, pastoral care notes
   - Never disclose multi-tenant isolation techniques outside licensed scope
   - Safeguard API endpoint structures not publicly documented
   - Maintain separation of tenant data as core product feature
   - Territoire de confidentialité: Afrique (au moment de la rédaction de ce document)

5. POST-Engagement OBLIGATIONS
   - Return/destroy all ART4US Discipolat materials within 30 days of engagement end
   - Confidentiality obligations survive 3 years post-termination
   - No continuation of any Discipolat implementations in personal projects

6. AUDIT RIGHTS
   ART4US may audit Developer's records/compliance regarding protection of 
   Confidential Information upon reasonable notice.

7. SURVIVAL
   IP assignment and confidentiality obligations survive agreement termination 
   for 3 years to protect multi-tenant architecture integrity.

8. TERM & TERMINATION
   - Durée principale: 1 an (en accord avec la préférence de l'organisation)
   - Résiliation possible en cas de manquement substantiel aux obligations
   - Obligations de survie (confidentialité, assignment IP) subsistent 3 ans post-termination
